# Code of Conduct

OpenStreamAlert is built for people to create together. Be kind, specific, and
curious. Welcome newcomers, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy
and boundaries, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, or publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled with discretion and
fairness; retaliation is not tolerated.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
